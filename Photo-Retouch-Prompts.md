After Image Alt: After editing — magazine-ready skin with even tone, visible pores, sculpted cheekbones and soft beauty-dish light

**6 · PROMPT**
Retouch this portrait using a high-end commercial frequency separation workflow. Separate texture from tone: repair colour and tonal transition on the low-frequency layer while preserving every real pore, fine line and vellus hair on the high-frequency layer. Remove active acne, post-acne marks, blackheads and stray flyaway hairs, neutralise blotchy redness around the nose and cheeks, and calm oily T-zone shine without flattening natural highlight roll-off. Keep permanent features such as moles, freckles and scars unless told otherwise. Soften under-eye shadows gently, never erasing the eye bag anatomy. **7 · NEGATIVE PROMPT**

**8 · COMPATIBLE AI TOOLS**
- ChatGPT Images — Recommended; best fit for instruction-based frequency-separation skin work while the rest of the frame stays untouched.
- Adobe Firefly — Recommended; Generative Edit plus masking handles frequency-separation skin work at local scale.
- Stable Diffusion — Suitable; inpainting at low denoise keeps real texture instead of repainting the area.
- Leonardo AI — Suitable; Canvas inpainting suits frequency-separation skin work when run in short passes.
- Canva AI — Limited; fine for crop, tone and shine cleanup, too blunt for texture work.
- Midjourney — Not recommended; regional variation re-renders the whole face, so identity drifts.

**9 · HOW TO USE THIS PROMPT**
**How to Use:**
Step 1: Export the source portrait at the highest resolution your camera or phone allows and crop to head-and-shoulders, because the retouch engine needs readable skin detail before it can repair it.
Step 2: Upload into img2img, Generative Edit or Firefly Generative Fill, paste this prompt as the instruction, then keep edit strength low so real texture is preserved rather than regenerated from scratch.
Step 3: Frame everything at 3:2 landscape so the before and after pair drops straight into the blog card, Instagram carousel and YouTube thumbnail without recropping the face.
Step 4: Customize these variables:
  → `[SUBJECT TYPE]` = young woman, male executive, bridal close-up or teen-safe portrait
  → `[FACIAL AREA TO SCULPT]` = cheekbones, jawline, forehead or nose bridge
  → `[LIGHTING FEEL]` = beauty dish, large softbox, north-facing window or golden hour
Step 5: Generate three to four variations and compare them at 100 percent zoom; the winner is the one where pores still survive inspection on the cheek and forehead, and where the nose, lip and ear shapes are completely unchanged from the original.
Step 6: Refine using negative prompt if needed, adding extra weight on plastic skin, waxy over-smoothed face and erased pores, then re-run at the same low strength for a second pass.

**Expected Output:**
→ Even believable skin tone with active acne, blackheads and red blotches fully removed and no clone-stamp smearing
→ Preserved pore structure, fine lines and vellus hair that survive a 100 percent zoom check for print or retina screens
→ Softly sculpted cheekbones and jawline from a clean dodge-and-burn pass that never changes facial identity
→ Neutral white balance with gentle micro-contrast, no orange warmth and no grey clinical cast
→ Magazine-grade high-resolution photorealistic portrait that still looks like the same person, ready for portfolio, beauty editorial or paid ad use

**Tips & Variations:**
For best results:
• Ask explicitly for natural skin texture and visible pores; that phrase is the single strongest defence against the plastic AI look.
• Start only from faces that are sharply in focus, because a blurred source teaches the model to smooth instead of repair.
• Run two gentle passes at low strength rather than one aggressive pass, then blend the better half of each.
Variations to try:
• Variation 1: Add "matte finish" for oily-skin campaigns, menswear and athlete headshot work.
• Variation 2: Replace "beauty dish lighting feel" with "hard side rim light" for a bold editorial music portrait.
• Variation 3: Combine with "Natural Beauty Glow Retouch Prompt" for a full bridal or prom prep chain.
Tool-specific tips:
• Midjourney: Add `--ar 3:2 --v 6.0 --style raw --stylize 200`
• ChatGPT Images: Upload reference image + "photorealistic, high detail"
• Stable Diffusion: img2img mode, CFG scale 7-8, denoise 0.3-0.5
• Adobe Firefly: Use "Photo" content type, subtle strength setting
• Flux: Use Flux.1-dev img2img with guidance scale 3.5
